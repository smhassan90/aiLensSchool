Privacy Policy

HawkNexa Parent mobile application

Effective date: September 22, 2026
Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HawkNexa ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the HawkNexa Parent mobile application (the "App") on Android devices. The App helps parents and guardians access school-related information for their enrolled children, such as homework, quizzes, attendance, fees, announcements, and notifications.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who this App is for

The App is intended for parents and legal guardians who have been given login credentials by their child's school. Schools configure accounts and link children to parent accounts. The App is not intended for use by children under 13 without parental supervision and school authorization.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Account and profile information

  • Username and password (used to authenticate you)
  • Name, email address, phone number, and role (parent/guardian)
  • School affiliation and linked student profiles assigned by the school

School and student-related information

When you use the App, we display information provided by your child's school, which may include:

  • Student name, class, section, and enrollment details
  • Homework, lessons, quizzes, and academic results
  • Attendance records and day-off requests
  • Fee statements, payment status, and receipts
  • Announcements, events, diary entries, and report cards
  • In-app notifications related to school activity

Device and technical information

  • Push notification token (FCM device token) when you enable notifications
  • Device platform information (e.g., Android) needed to deliver notifications
  • Authentication tokens stored securely on your device
  • Basic app usage data needed to operate and secure the service (e.g., API request logs)
The App does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location to function. We do not collect GPS location data through the App.

3. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Authenticate you and provide access to your linked children's school records
  • Display homework, quizzes, attendance, fees, announcements, and other school content
  • Send push notifications about school updates when you opt in
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, and troubleshoot technical issues
  • Improve reliability and performance of the App and related services
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

4. How information is stored

  • On your device: Login tokens and your selected child preference are stored using secure storage on your device.
  • On our servers: Account and school data is processed on servers operated for HawkNexa and accessed through hawknexabackend.fynals.com.

5. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — to deliver push notifications on Android. Google may process device tokens according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — to securely host our backend and web services.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

6. Data sharing

We may share information only in these situations:

  • With your child's school and authorized school staff, as required to provide the service
  • With service providers who help us operate the App under confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights and safety
  • With your consent or at your direction

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Schools may retain academic and administrative records according to their own policies. When you log out, authentication tokens on your device are cleared. Push notification tokens may be removed when you disable notifications or uninstall the App.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or update your account information
  • Request deletion of your account, subject to school and legal record-keeping requirements
  • Disable push notifications in your device settings or within the App settings
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Because schools create and manage parent accounts, many requests (such as account updates or deletion) should be directed to your child's school administrator. We will assist schools in fulfilling valid requests where applicable.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure authentication tokens, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

The App allows parents and guardians to view information about their children as authorized by the school. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through the App. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without appropriate authorization, please contact us or your school so we can take appropriate action.

11. International users

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in locations with different data protection laws.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we may provide additional notice through the App or your school.
