Unit 10: General Wave properties
10.3 Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)
Periodic Motion
A motion repeating itself in an equal time interval is referred to as periodic or oscillatory motion.
Simple Harmonic Motion
When an object oscillates about a fixed position (mean position) its acceleration is directly proportional to its displacement from the mean position and is always directed towards the mean position.
a ∝ -x
f = -(k/m)x
The restoring force is a force which acts to bring a body to its equilibrium position.
10.4 Simple pendulum
Forces acting on a displaced pendulum
i. Tension T along the direction of the string
ii. Weight W = mg acting vertically downwards
For SHM: T = 2π√(L/g)
The period of a simple pendulum depends upon its length and acceleration due to gravity.
