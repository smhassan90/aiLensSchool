10.3 Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)
Periodic Motion
A motion repeating itself in an equal time interval is referred to as periodic or oscillatory motion.
Simple Harmonic Motion
When an object oscillates about a fixed position (mean position) its acceleration is directly proportional to its displacement from the mean position.
