Unit 10: General Wave properties
10.1 Waves and nature of waves
A method transport energy from one point to another point without transfer of matter is called wave.
Formation of waves
Disturbances in a medium cause the formation of waves using a rope, a slinky spring, and water waves in ripple tanks.
Wave Motion by using a Rope
Attaching one end of a rope to a wall and moving the other end up and down produces oscillations or vibrations.
