Worked Example 4
Pendulum clocks with a pendulum measuring out the passing of a second. How long of a pendulum is required to have a period of 1 second? g = 9.8 m/s².
Solution:
Step 1: Write down the known quantities and quantities to be found.
L = ?
T = 1.0 s
g = 9.8 m/s²
Step 2: Write down the formula and rearrange if necessary.
T = 2π√(L/g)
Square both the sides
L = (T²g) / (4π²)
Step 3: Put the values and calculate
L = 0.25m
Result: The length of a pendulum should be 0.25 m.
